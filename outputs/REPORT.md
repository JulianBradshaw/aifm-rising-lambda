# Two-λ variant (daniel-08-16-26, native present day)

One λ (the preset’s 0.5) for history and calibration, a separate forward λ for coding labor from today on. Today’s automation fraction, AI labor and r_software are therefore the same at every forward λ. The single-λ rows are the unmodified model with that λ everywhere. Takeoff is AC to ASI in months; deterministic single runs at preset medians, not forecasts. Regenerate with `python -m two_lambda`.
