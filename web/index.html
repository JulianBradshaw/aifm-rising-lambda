How to read this

  • λ is the return to parallel coding labor: doubling it multiplies serial labor by 2λ. The original model uses one λ for historical human teams and future AI swarms alike.
  • With one λ, raising it also changes two calibrations of the past: today's measured coding uplift is read as less automation, and returns to software research (r) are refit to 2024 progress. Both lengthen takeoff and can outweigh the direct benefit.
  • The two-λ variant keeps both calibrations at λ 0.5, so today's automation and r are unchanged, and applies the forward λ only to growth in coding labor after today.
  • AC is the automated coder and ASI artificial superintelligence, as defined by the AI Futures Model. Takeoff is the time from AC to ASI. Coding Uplift anchor.
  • The original model accepts λ up to 1. Everything is solved in your browser with Pyodide. Source and method: aifm-lambda-fixes.
